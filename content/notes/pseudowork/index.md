## What's It?

"**Psuedowork: How We Ended Up Being Busy Doing Nothing**" is a book by anthropologist *Dennis Nørmark* and philosopher *Anders Fogh Jensen*.

It examines why modern workers stay busy with low-value or pointless tasks despite efficiency gains and technology.

## Why It Happens?

In this book, Dennis and Anders Fogh Jensen set out to discover how we spend our working lives. 
It is a journey into absurdity, where the meaning of work has disappeared and the promise of leisure has never been fulfilled. 

> Instead, we have more rules, useless projects, forgettable HR initiatives, endless meetings and trivial PowerPoint presentations.

The authors come from both sides of the political divide, but this book is not a meeting in the middle. 

It’s a showdown with an old-fashioned concept of work, and a blueprint for what we can do about it – as employees, as managers and as a society.

It is time to think and act differently. 
Otherwise, we may find ourselves committing the greatest act of self-sabotage in history. 

We risk making a mockery of our past and being seen as a laughing stock in the future. 
First, we must confront one of the greatest taboos of our era: Pseudowork.

## Core Ideas

 - **What pseudowork is:** Activities that imitate real work or look busy without creating actual value for clients, citizens, or products. *If stopped, no one would miss them.*

 - **Common examples:** Endless meetings, unread reports, trivial PowerPoint presentations, excess rules, bureaucratic registrations, and performative HR or management routines.

 - **Why it persists:** Leaders can become detached from frontline work, organizations add rules and processes to handle small issues, and employees equate visible busyness with job security.
 - **The unfulfilled leisure promise:** Citing ideas like John Maynard Keynes's 1930 prediction of a 15-hour workweek, the authors argue that productivity gains often get absorbed into busywork rather than shorter hours or deeper value.

 - **How to push back:** The book offers practical steps for employees, managers, and organizations to spot low-value tasks, cut administrative clutter, and focus on meaningful output.

## Book Contents

> Here are the English translations of all the chapter titles from ***Pseudowork: How We Ended Up Being Busy Doing Nothing*** (*Pseudoarbejde*) by Dennis Nørmark and Anders Fogh Jensen:

**Prologue (序章)**

**Part I: Disappearing Time (第一篇：消失的時間)**

: Examines the unfulfilled prediction of a 15-hour workweek despite massive automation and efficiency gains.
: Classifies the six main types of pseudowork (such as having nothing to do, bad IT systems, pointless reporting, copying other organizations, bureaucracy, and *leadership bullshit*).

* **Chapter 1:** The Future Isn't What It Used to Be (未來已不復昔日模樣)
* **Chapter 2:** The Rotten Core of the Workplace System (職場體制中的腐朽核心)
* **Chapter 3:** Pseudomorphs of Work (勞動的偽形)

**Part II: Fading Meaning (第二篇：消逝的意義)**

: Explores the root causes of why pseudowork expands, such as the shift from measurable "front stage" work to "backstage" administrative roles, over-reliance on KPIs, decision avoidance, and fake employee involvement.
: Analyzes the consequences on employee morale, self-respect, and workplace resistance to change.

* **Chapter 4:** Just Go Home! (那就回家吧！)
* **Chapter 5:** Don't Invent Solutions (不要發明解決方案)
* **Chapter 6:** Stop Blind Imitation (停止盲目模仿)
* **Chapter 7:** Keep Your Feet on the Ground (腳踏實地)
* **Chapter 8:** Curb Blind Positive Thinking (抑制盲目的正向思考)
* **Chapter 9:** Stop Interrupting Work (別再打斷工作了)
* **Chapter 10:** Stop Paying by the Hour (別再按時間給薪)
* **Chapter 11:** Trust Others (信任他人)


**Part III: Reclaiming Time and Meaning (第三篇：重拾時間與意義)**

: Outlines a blueprint for employees, managers, and society to eliminate pointless tasks, such as paying for results rather than hours and disconnecting value from time spent.

* **Chapter 12:** Why Do We Work? (我們為何而勞動？)
* **Chapter 13:** What Can You Do? (你可以做些什麼？)
* **Chapter 14:** What Can Managers Do? (主管能做些什麼？)
* **Chapter 15:** A World Without Pseudowork (沒有偽勞動的世界)
