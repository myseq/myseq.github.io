Cloudflare unveiled a repo, **Zero Wire**[^1], dedicated to tracking critical software vulnerabilities. 

The platform operates within the security division of the public **Cloudflare Radar**[^2] threat intelligence portal.
The objective is to aggregate actionalbe threat intelligence across the global Internet.

[^1]: https://radar.cloudflare.com/security/zero-wire
[^2]: https://radar.cloudflare.com/about

## Zero Wire

**Zero Wire** includes vulnerability briefings for modern web applications, software runtimes, underlying dependencies, and outward-facing infrastructure components.

Each briefing outlines affected software components, remediation strategies, and supporting evidence.
It can connect the technical issue to its practical consequences, such as service disruption or risk of data exposure.

## Automted AI Prompt

Beyond static technical briefings, it also *delivers* structured precompiled prompt[^3] for AI agent.

This means an autonomous agents can examine deployed software versions, vendor patches, external exposure vectors, and firewall policies, and generate a comprehensive compliance evidence and tailored remediation.

[^3]: https://blog.cloudflare.com/zero-wire/#assess-your-exposure-with-an-ai-agent
